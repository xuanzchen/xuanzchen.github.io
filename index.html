About Me

I am a senior in bioinformatics at the School of Life Sciences, Peking University, pursuing a double degree in economics at the National School of Development.

My research focuses on spatial multi-omics, machine learning for biology, and scientific agents. In Hu Zeng's lab at Peking University, I work on imaging-based spatial transcriptomics and translatomics, from image processing to downstream analysis. In Xiaojie Qiu's lab at Stanford, I contributed to Bio-Babel and am training a model for joint molecular and cellular inference from spatial transcriptomics images.

I am also developing iST Agent for reliable execution of imaging-based spatial transcriptomics workflows. Since October 2026, I have been a remote research intern at Canchen Technology, mentored by Lei Li, with a focus on agentic world models for virtual tissue.

Outside research, I enjoy playing volleyball.

Selected Work

GitHub profile ↗
  1. In progress

    Spatial Multi-omics Analysis of GBM with STATES

    STATES jointly profiles spatial transcriptomes and translatomes. I benchmarked cell-segmentation methods, integrated 3D Cellpose nuclear segmentation into ClusterMap, and improved image stitching. The proportion of seams misaligned by more than 3 pixels fell from 14.2% to 0.3%; multi-nucleus segments fell from 28.8% to 13–14% against the same nuclear reference. My downstream work examines malignant-cell identities, subtypes, and spatial variation in translation efficiency in glioblastoma.

    Spatial multi-omics · Spatial oncology · GBM

  2. Preprint · bioRxiv 2026

    Bio-Babel

    Bio-Babel reconstructs computational biology software across languages for use by scientific agents. I built an annotation agent that generates machine-readable usage contracts for reconstructed packages, extended coding-agent compatibility, and developed tooling for agent-driven Python-to-C++ reconstruction. In the pancreatic differentiation case study, I prepared and annotated the data, reviewed analyses executed by Bio-Babel's agents, and completed genotype-aware screening and biological interpretation of reduced maturation signatures under SWI/SNF loss. Code: pancreas case study, UMI-tools-cpp.

    Scientific agents · Software translation · Research software

  3. Qiu Lab · In progress

    End-to-End Spatial Transcriptomics Modeling

    I am developing and training a model on public MERFISH data that couples molecular localization and barcode decoding with cell segmentation and molecule-to-cell assignment. It combines RNA fluorescence and cellular morphology, learning cross-round correspondences without a separate image-warping stage. The project tests whether sharing evidence across these interdependent tasks can reduce errors introduced by sequential processing.

    Deep learning · iST · Multimodal learning · Bioimage analysis

  4. Peking University · Undergraduate thesis

    iST Agent

    Inspired by Bio-Babel, I built an agent for reliable execution of heterogeneous imaging-based spatial transcriptomics workflows. It separates usage contracts derived from source code from memory of runtime failures and fixes. I generated contracts for seven pipelines and reproduced one pipeline's decoding byte for byte. In one comparison, preflight checks caught a multi-sample configuration defect with memory but missed it without memory.

    Scientific agents · iST · Bioimage processing · Workflow orchestration
